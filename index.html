Authorship

Twenty-five years listening. A lifetime telling stories.

Nancy meandered professionally through nursing, perinatal education, and marriage & family therapy until she found her true calling in education. Being a resource coordinator, a life skills counselor, and a high school counselor defined her and unleashed her passion for guiding, nurturing, supporting, and listening to adolescents transition into adulthood. She's grateful for every student who shared their life with her, trusting the safe space she created for each of them.

That same steadiness now shows up in two places: on the page, in her award-winning book, and in the recording booth, where her clear, confident, and soothing voice brings scripts, stories, and public messages to life. She doesn't simply read her lines — she presents them with a conviction that leaves a real impression.

"

I shared so much laughter, tears, highs, lows, angst, anticipation, and wonder with my students. This is a large part of why I wrote the book: as a tribute to them, and to help others — counselors, teachers, administrators, parents, and non-educators — find the passion in their own lives.

— Nancy Regas

The Book

The Art of Being a
School Counselor

Leading with Confidence, Compassion, and Authenticity

Drawing on twenty-five years in public education and counseling, Nancy L. Regas, M.S., M.F.T., P.P.S., writes for the counselors, teachers, and parents standing in the hallway with a student who needs them. Part memoir, part field guide, it's a book about doing this work with steadiness and heart — and about trusting your own voice when it matters most.

Using her Velvety Vocal Vibes, Nancy narrated the audiobook herself — the same warm, grounded voice her students, clients, and voiceover clients know well. That performance earned the audiobook a Distinguished Favorite honor at the NYC Big Book Awards. You can hear a preview in the Voice Work section below.

Voice Work

The Velvety Vocal Vibes of Nancy Regas

Capture your audience with a clear, confident, soothing, and engaging voice — as if she's speaking only to you. Nancy has experience in public speaking, radio commercials, e-learning, and online advertising.

Voiceover Work

No matter the copy, it's delivered with a clear, confident, soothing voice. Past clients include Science Media, Cubicle Ninjas, Teva Pharmaceuticals, Catholic Strong, Sun Harbor Barbershop Chorus, Seacrest Nursery, and Starlust Jewelry — plus commercials for Tasti Lee Tomatoes, Sasha's Canine Bakery, Thrift Pets, and Vintage Gold Classics.

Public Speaking

A public educator and counselor for twenty-five years, Nancy is well versed on teen issues — making the most of high school, the college search, teen/parent dialogue, and teen relationship violence. Her audiences have included parents, students, teachers, high school counselors, and churches.
